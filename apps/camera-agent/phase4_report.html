8 · Disconnect / reconnect — PASS (one documented finding)

Live unplug/replug test: while unplugged, /status kept connected:true (stale DeviceManager enumeration) with resolution:null and /preview → 409 — typed, recoverable, no crash. After replug: full recovery within seconds, no restart. Contrast: Phase 3's involuntary USB-bus drop correctly showed connected:false + 503.

Documented finding (not fixed — scope): a clean unplug surfaces the busy-style message instead of "disconnected" until the driver refreshes enumeration. Future work: re-enumerate on open-failure to distinguish vanished-device from busy.
